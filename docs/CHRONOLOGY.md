## Perceived chronology **[P]** — what each witness will tell you

| Witness | Claimed last contact | Why they believe it |
|---|---|---|
| **Trent** (Chronos) | 11:42:44 | He *did* hear "Confirmed" at that moment. He is closest to the truth of anyone on the mountain, and it is the least useful accurate statement in the game. |
| **Rook** (Mechanical) | 11:44:05 | Heard "Proceed to sequence three" and started the sequence. **The motor current spike begins 1.2 s before the phrase arrives** — because the relay delayed the audio, not the order. |
| **Okafor** (Aurora) | 11:46:45 | Her station clock carries Trent's hidden **+4.8 s**, so her log says 11:46:45 for an event at 11:46:40. She is also the only one who thought the reply sounded wrong. |
| **Cross** (Annex) | 11:49:30 | Heard "Stand by." Assumed Coll was busy. |
| **Voss** (Spectra) | 11:48:02 | Claims she was on the channel when Coll said "I have it. Beautiful." She is describing a line she manufactured. |

Every one of them is telling the truth about what they heard. That is the trap:
the player will spend Act II looking for a liar, and there isn't one.

### The discrepancy budget

| Source of error | Magnitude |
|---|---|
| Trent's concealed correction on the Aurora feed | 4.8 s |
| Emergency relay buffering under load | ~11 s |
| Human recollection of eclipse phase vs clock time | ±60 s |
| **Prerecorded and synthetic audio** | **~8 minutes** |

The first three are real and the player will find them first. They account for
nothing. That is the point at which the mystery turns.
