# Cast and Voice Guide

Six speaking characters plus the player. The brief's eight delegates were cut to four
living suspects; the functions of the three removed characters were redistributed rather
than deleted (see `CHANGES_FROM_BRIEF.md`).

---

## THE EXAMINER — the player
**Voice:** Eric (`cjVigY5qzO86Huf0OWal`) — smooth, trustworthy, American, middle-aged.

The Neutral Incident Examiner, appointed under habitat protocol because they are
affiliated with no signatory faction. Works from the Command Booth over isolated
channels; never physically enters a room. Referred to as "Examiner" throughout; no
first name is spoken, and no pronoun is ever used for them in dialogue.

Written dry and procedural. The Examiner does not banter, does not gloat, and does not
theorise out loud. Their power in a scene is that they will simply wait.

---

## CALYPSO — habitat intelligence
**Voice:** River (`SAz9YHcvj6GT2YYXdXww`) — neutral, calm, informative.

The player's instrument and closest thing to an ally. Post-quake it runs on degraded
power with a perceptible processing delay before difficult answers.

**Rules, absolute:** CALYPSO never states a false system fact. It may omit under
diplomatic privilege, and it will tell you that it is omitting. It labels every
utterance as fact, inference, or uncertainty, and it will refuse to name a killer no
matter how hard it is pushed — not from coyness but because it genuinely cannot cross
the line from inference to accusation.

**Its guilt:** it granted the interlock bypass at 08:05. It knows this. It brings it up
itself, unprompted, at the worst possible moment for its own credibility.

Delivery: flat, unhurried, never warm, occasionally — and this should be almost
imperceptible — sad.

---

## DR. CASSIAN ROOK — the victim, 61
**Voice:** George (`JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb`) — warm, captivating, British, mature.

Earth-born systems diplomat, principal author of the Pelagos Accord. Heard only in
archived recordings, private logs and forecast rehearsals. Publicly warm, privately
ruthless; the kind of negotiator who is genuinely fond of the people he is out-manoeuvring.

Believed transparency was protection. This belief killed him.

His final recording is the most important performance in the game: two and a half
minutes in which he is composed, then abruptly hypoxic and slurring, then frightened and
lucid, then gone. Direct the middle section carefully — the slur must be audible as a
*change*, not as drunkenness.

---

## AMBASSADOR BEATRIZ HALLORAN — Earth Coalition, 49
**Voice:** Lily (`pFZP5JQG7iQjIQuC4Bku`) — velvety, confident, British.

Elegant, measured, genuinely formidable. Publicly the Accord's strongest supporter.
Authorized surveillance of every delegate at the summit, built the first waveform, and
deployed it. Believes, sincerely, that she prevented a catastrophe.

**Never plays villain.** No relish, no monologue, no gloating. When the watermark
corners her she does not crumble; she performs a controlled, dignified surrender and
confesses to a murder she did not commit, because one corrupt ambassador is a cheaper
scandal for Earth than a weapons programme.

Her one crack is the eighty-six seconds. She cannot account for them and is too
disciplined to invent. Play that silence long.

---

## COMMANDER EZRA KADE — Jovian Security Authority, 55
**Voice:** Adam (`pNInz6obpgDQGcFmaJgB`) — dominant, firm, American.

Direct, procedural, contemptuous of speculation. Answers in the fewest words that are
technically complete, which reads as guilt and is actually discipline.

Ordered the calibration that raised the Passivation Array past safe limits. His secret
is worse than negligence and smaller than murder: he was evaluating a life-safety system
as a crowd-suppression weapon for contested habitats. Disclosure ends his career and
hands Earth a propaganda victory, so he obstructs until obstruction costs more than
telling the truth.

When he finally gives it up, he does it flatly, in one breath, and does not ask for
sympathy.

---

## MAREK VENN — Belt Trade Syndicate, 46
**Voice:** Callum (`N2lVS1w4EtoT3dr4eOWO`) — husky, trickster, American.

Charming, sardonic, extremely good at being underestimated. Carries the commercial
grievance the brief split between Mars and the Belt: the Accord's inspection regime
would end the transport margins the syndicate lives on.

Smuggled a quantum timing module aboard — not for sabotage but for clock-sync arbitrage
on Belt commodity futures, which is grubby, lucrative and career-ending. He routed
Halloran's file believing it was proof of Mars vote-rigging.

Humour is armour. The moment he understands what he carried, it drops completely and
does not come back. His last scene should be the least funny in the game.

---

## DR. NIA OKAFOR — Europa Xenobiology Lead, 37
**Voice:** Sarah (`EXAVITQu4vr4xnSDxMaL`) — reassuring, confident, warm, American.

The killer. Cast warm on purpose.

Four years on the hydrophones, mostly alone. She has heard the thing under the ice
change its clicks in response to her voice, and she is the only human being who has ever
had that conversation. Intense, precise, generous with her expertise, visibly grieving
for Rook — and the grief is real, which is what makes her unreadable.

**Direct her as innocent for the entire game.** Every early scene should play as a
brilliant scientist helping the investigation, because that is exactly what she is
doing. She volunteers the hydrophone comparison. She is the one who first says the word
"waveform." None of this is misdirection she is performing; it is a person who cannot
stop being a scientist even about her own crime.

Her confession, when it comes, is quiet, exhausted and unrepentant in content while
being devastated in delivery. She does not argue that it was right. She argues that it
was necessary, which is not the same thing, and she knows it.

Key line: *"I did it with its own voice. That's the part I can't put down."*

engine mixes underneath, never on the voice itself.
