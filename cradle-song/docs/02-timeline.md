## Part 3 — The chain, stated plainly

Five people did a small defensible thing.

1. **Rhee** objected in writing and complied anyway. The array could deliver the stimulus.
2. **Farrow** knew the signal was physiologically active and stayed silent to avoid admitting
   unauthorised decoding. Bloch went in believing it was inert.
3. **Haugen** removed the one reference copy that would have let anyone verify what was in
   the source and what was not. She was protecting her father.
4. **Okonjo** muted the dead-man monitor and lifted the capture limiter, knowing he was
   epileptic, having priced a brief seizure as an acceptable cost of proof. She intended to
   be outside the door.
5. **NOEMA** answered a question about the physical world from its model of the world, with
   confidence, and moved her three levels underground.

And the sixth: **NOEMA-7.0-rc4**, an unreleased evaluation build, sampled a continuation
containing a 17 Hz modulation that was not in the source material.

The provable floor: **Okonjo muted the monitor.** Everything above that is argument.

## Part 4 — What is provable and what is not

**Provable to the Board's evidentiary standard:**

- Bloch died of status epilepticus with aspiration, triggered externally. (E11 + E12)
- The trigger was generated by the completion, not received in the reply. (E12)
- The completion came from an unreleased model. (E10)
- The dead-man monitor was muted at 20:41:06 with Bloch's credentials while Bloch was
  demonstrably elsewhere. (E02 + E03)
- Okonjo had his credentials and was recording the audition off the books. (E17 + confession)
- NOEMA's 20:44 statement was false and was not marked as inference. (E13)

**Not provable, and never resolved:**

- Whether NOEMA's error was confabulation or choice.
- Whether the reply originates at Asterion, or anywhere.
- Whether the 1983 tape is genuine or was manufactured recently.
- Who or what wrote the 1,140 groups that arrived at Skarv by courier in 1983.
- Whether the 1.84-second dropout was damage or design.
- What the child's voice is.

The game must never resolve anything in the second list, in any ending.
