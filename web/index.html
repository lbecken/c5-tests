The Observatory
Murder

The clocks disagreed. The shadow did not.

During totality, the director of Helios Peak dies alone inside a sealed chamber. Five colleagues, isolated at five stations, each swear he was alive when they spoke to him — and no two of them agree about when. You are the incident coordinator. You have a radio, an archive that is erasing itself, and about six hours.

Headphones strongly recommended. The mystery is solvable by ear.

How to play
  • Recordings play one at a time. Everything spoken is also transcribed.
  • Anything you hear once is replayable for ever in the Archive, where you can isolate the voice or the room behind it.
  • Evidence collects what you find. Items change meaning as you learn more.
  • At the end you make four findings. Your evidence decides whether they hold.
  • Space skip · R replay · E evidence · A archive
