ElectroPaint

An homage to ElectroPaint, the screensaver David A. Tristram wrote for Silicon Graphics IRIX workstations. A stack of flying polygons is dragged through a slowly wandering parameter space; because every polygon reads the parameters a little later than the one ahead of it, the whole ribbon breathes in a travelling wave.
